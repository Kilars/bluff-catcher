# Knowledge-base channels

The channels `kb-nightly` lists videos from. To add or drop one, edit the
table. The handle is the part after `youtube.com/@`. Picked and checked on
2026-10-09 (see `nightly-kb-plan.md`, decision 10).

| Handle | Coach | Role | Filter note |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTOWizard | GTO Wizard (various) | GTO baseline (authoritative) | drop table highlights, product promos, MTT |
| UriPelegPoker | Uri Peleg | online cash liveplay 50NL–1kNL, solver explainers | closest to the user's game |
| RedChipPoker | Red Chip Poker (James Sweeney, Ed Miller, others) | low-stakes 6-max fundamentals, exploits | tag exploits by population; drop podcast chat episodes |
| Upswingpoker | Upswing Poker (various) | preflop and fundamentals | heavy PLO and MTT mix, filter hard |
| PokerCoaching | Jonathan Little | fundamentals (math, hand classes) | top views are entertainment and MTT, filter hard |
